Open & click tracking is off by default. Turn it on per mailbox (button on the right of each row) to see opens and CTR on Analytics — it adds a 1×1 pixel and wraps links in outgoing HTML. Two cautions: pixels and link-wrapping are cold-email spam signals, so serve tracking links from a subdomain of your sending domain (set APP_BASE_URL, e.g. https://track.yourdomain.com), never a raw tunnel/onrender URL for real sending; and opens are directional (Apple Mail & Gmail pre-load images) — trust clicks and replies.
