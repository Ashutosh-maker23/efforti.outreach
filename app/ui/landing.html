The workflow

Four moves, start to booked.

Nothing here is a black box. Every step is a page you can open, inspect and override — the engine does the repetitive part, you keep the judgment.

1

Source

Pull decision-makers from Apollo by role, industry, HQ and headcount. Off-ICP contacts are dropped before a credit is spent.

2

Personalize

Every lead is ICP-scored and given an opener written from its own company facts — not a mail-merge of first names.

3

Send

One opener plus four in-thread follow-ups, spread across your mailboxes on a working-day cadence. Weekends are never touched.

4

Convert

Replies are polled straight from your inbox and stop the sequence instantly. Opens, clicks and demos land on one dashboard.

IMAP polling reads your real inbox. A reply halts that lead's sequence, a bounce suppresses the address, an unsubscribe is honored on the spot — no manual cleanup.

Deliverability

Built to stay out of the spam folder.

Cold email only works while it lands. The engine is opinionated about the things that quietly destroy a domain — and it never makes those choices for you silently.

Lookalike domains only

Send from getefforti.com or tryefforti.com — never the domain your product and invoices live on. A burned sending domain stays a sending domain.

Warm-up guidance, never a hard cap

Around 8 sends on day one, then +2 a day, is the recommendation shown in the app. It is guidance you can read — nothing is silently throttled behind your back.

Honest about tracking

Pixels and wrapped links are spam signals, so tracking ships off and is enabled per mailbox — served from your own subdomain, with the caveats stated plainly.
