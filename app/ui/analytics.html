About the data. Everything here is first-party. Replies come from polling your own mailboxes over IMAP; opens and clicks come from a 1×1 pixel and wrapped links on emails sent from tracking-enabled mailboxes (turn tracking on per mailbox on the Mailboxes page). Opens are directional — Apple Mail Privacy Protection and Gmail's image proxy pre-load the pixel, inflating opens and even back-filling one on a click — so weight clicks and replies more heavily. Bounce and unsubscribe are captured automatically at send and poll time.
